# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Showing patience with newcomers

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Insulting, derogatory, or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing another person's private information without permission

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any public space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to iamgautamsuthar@gmail.com. Reports are reviewed in confidence and acted on as the maintainers judge appropriate, up to and including a permanent ban from the project.
